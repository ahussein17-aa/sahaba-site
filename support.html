Content corrections

The stories in Sahaba are drawn from classical sirah and hadith literature, and narrations of uncertain authenticity are hedged in the text. If you believe something is inaccurate or insufficiently sourced, please email with the profile name and section title — corrections are taken seriously and shipped in updates.

Common questions

Does the app need an internet connection? No. All content is included with the app and everything works offline.

Where are my saved reflections? In the Saved tab. They are stored only on your device — see the privacy policy.

How do I add the widgets? Long-press your home screen, tap the + button, and search for "Sahaba" to add the Companion of the Day or Daily Reflection widget.

The daily notification isn't arriving. Check Settings → Notifications → Sahaba on your iPhone, and make sure Focus modes allow it.
